# Standard Maintenance Repair Procedures

---
title: Corrective Action for Severe Bearing Degradation (P1/P2)
machine_type: all
document_type: procedure
component: bearing
failure_type: bearing_wear
section: Bearing Replacement Protocol SOP-MECH-04
---
When a machine exhibits Health State 2 (Critical) or RUL < 24 hours with bearing degradation:
1. Lockout/Tagout (LOTO): Isolate motor drive electrical supply and depressurize process piping.
2. Decouple machine from driver and measure cold shaft runout and alignment baseline.
3. Remove bearing housing cap and inspect grease condition for metallic debris or burnt odor.
4. Extract damaged bearing using mechanical puller or induction heater. Never use cutting torches near precision shaft journals.
5. Clean journal surfaces with lint-free solvent and micrometer-check seat diameter for fretting wear.
6. Heat replacement bearing uniformly to 110°C using an eddy-current induction heater. Slide squarely onto shaft against shoulder.
7. Fill bearing cavities 30% to 50% with approved synthetic/mineral grease. Replace housing seals.
8. Realign coupling to within 0.05 mm parallel and 0.03 mm/100mm angular tolerance before restart.

---
title: Dynamic In-Situ Rotor Balancing Procedure
machine_type: all
document_type: procedure
component: rotor
failure_type: unbalance
section: Rotor Balancing Protocol SOP-MECH-12
---
When radial vibration (vibration_x / vibration_y) shows dominant 1X unbalance:
1. Perform safety visual inspection of rotor, impellers, or fan blades for cake accumulation or erosion.
2. Clean all impeller surfaces thoroughly with pressure wash or wire brush and inspect balance weights.
3. Attach optical tachometer trigger and trial mass of known weight at standard polar angle.
4. Execute single-plane or two-plane dynamic run; measure trial vibration response and compute correction weights.
5. Weld or bolt permanent stainless balance weight at designated angular position.
6. Verify trim run confirms overall vibration velocity is restored below 0.8 mm/s.

---
title: Precision Shaft Laser Alignment Procedure
machine_type: all
document_type: procedure
component: shaft_coupling
failure_type: misalignment
section: Laser Alignment Protocol SOP-ALIGN-02
---
When elevated axial vibration (Z-axis) indicates angular or offset misalignment:
1. Verify machine feet are clean and check for soft foot condition (foot lift < 0.05 mm under torque release).
2. Mount dual-head laser alignment sensors across coupling hubs.
3. Rotate shafts through 180 degrees minimum and record vertical and horizontal offset and angular deviations.
4. Calculate required stainless steel shim thickness adjustments under front and back motor feet.
5. Apply shims, tighten hold-down bolts to specified torque in diagonal pattern.
6. Confirm re-measurement satisfies ISO precision tolerance (offset < 0.05 mm, angularity < 0.05 mm/100mm).
