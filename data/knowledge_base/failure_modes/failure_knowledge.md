# Mechanical Failure Knowledge Base

title: Rolling Element Bearing Wear and Flaking
machine_type: all
document_type: failure_mode
component: bearing
failure_type: bearing_wear
section: Rolling Element Fatigue & Spalling
---
Bearing degradation progresses through four distinct stages:
Stage 1: Micro-surface subsurface fissures generating high frequency ultrasonic stress waves.
Stage 2: Pitting and spalling on bearing raceways. Vibration harmonics appear in the 500 Hz to 2 kHz band. Overall vibration RMS increases from 0.8 to 1.2 mm/s.
Stage 3: Severe spalling across inner/outer race and rolling balls. Harmonics modulate rotation speed 1X and 2X sidebands. Temperature increases rapidly (+10°C to +25°C above ambient) due to rolling friction.
Stage 4: Complete cage fracture, ball skidding, thermal binding. Overall vibration spikes dramatically above 2.0 mm/s. Immediate seizure hazard within 0 to 12 operating hours.

title: Dynamic Rotor Unbalance
machine_type: all
document_type: failure_mode
component: rotor
failure_type: unbalance
section: Centrifugal Unbalance Symptoms
---
Rotor unbalance occurs when the mass centerline deviates from the geometric rotational axis due to particulate buildup, blade erosion, or loss of balance weights.
Symptoms:
- Strong 1X rotational frequency peak on radial sensors (vibration_x and vibration_y).
- 90 degree phase shift between horizontal (X) and vertical (Y) vibration transducers.
- Low axial vibration (Z-axis).
- Little to no immediate temperature increase unless excessive radial load forces bearing friction.

---
title: Shaft Misalignment (Angular and Parallel)
machine_type: all
document_type: failure_mode
component: shaft_coupling
failure_type: misalignment
section: Coupling Misalignment Signatures
---
Misalignment occurs between driver motor and driven equipment shafts across flexible or rigid couplings.
Symptoms:
- High 2X rotational speed vibration component.
- Elevated axial vibration (vibration_z), frequently exceeding 50% of radial horizontal vibration.
- 180 degree phase difference across the coupling interface.
- Accelerated wear on mechanical seals, coupling elastomeric inserts, and drive-end bearings.
- Localized coupling hub heating exceeding 75°C.

---
title: Lubrication Starvation and Thermal Breakdown
machine_type: all
document_type: failure_mode
component: lubrication_system
failure_type: overheating
section: Boundary Lubrication & Oil Oxidation
---
Insufficient oil volume, low grease replenishment, or viscous thermal shearing causes boundary metal-to-metal contact.
Symptoms:
- Rapid temperature escalation (> 80°C) preceding significant low-frequency vibration rise.
- Acoustic emission high-frequency hiss followed by rapid RMS vibration spike.
- Darkened, oxidized grease or varnish formation inside bearing caps.
