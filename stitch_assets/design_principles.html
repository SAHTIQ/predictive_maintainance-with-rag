UI Design Principles & System Specification

The official technical standard and design framework for Resonex plant monitoring interfaces. Built for extreme information density, sub-second cognitive intake, and fail-safe industrial dispatching.

Foundations

1. Core Operational Tenets

Mechanical technicians and monitoring supervisors operate under continuous cognitive strain. Every layout choice must serve triage speed and precision.

grid_view

High-Density Operational Clarity

Present live telemetry across 50+ concurrent turbine and pump trains without visual clutter. Employs micro-gutters, structured progressive disclosure, and tabular alignment to compress 100Hz vibration curves.

Rule: Zero Ornamental Canvas
notifications_paused

Alert Fatigue Elimination

Strict color governance where crimson (#ef4444) is strictly locked for immediate P1 trip hazards (RUL <12h). Sub-threshold anomalies must cluster automatically into unified single-machine units.

Rule: Correlate Multi-Signal Alerts
fact_check

Context-Bound Actionability

Never render naked sensor alerts without an immediately executable mitigation path: tied standard operating procedure (SOP), technician dispatch button, and Lock-Out Tag-Out (LOTO) protocols.

Rule: Never Alert Without Next Step
cognition

Verified AI Transparency

AI diagnostics must rigorously isolate raw sensor telemetry, mathematical FFT calculations, OEM manuals, and generative predictions. Fabricated statistical certainties are strictly blocked.

Rule: Explicit Attribution Layers
