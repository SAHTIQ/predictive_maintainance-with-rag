machine_type: TypeA
document_type: manual
component: pump_casing_and_bearings
section: General Operating Limits & Lubrication
---
The TypeA industrial pump is an axially split centrifugal unit designed for continuous slurry transfer.
Nominal rotational speed operating band is 1450 to 1800 RPM.
Operating temperature must remain below 70°C for standard lithium grease lubrication.
When operating vibration exceeds 1.2 mm/s RMS on any axis (X, Y, or Z), the machine must be inspected for hydrodynamic cavitation and loose pedestal hold-down bolts.
Bearing re-greasing interval is 500 operating hours using ISO VG 100 mineral grease. Over-lubrication causes churning and thermal runaway.

title: TypeB Multi-Stage Centrifugal Compressor Manual
machine_type: TypeB
document_type: manual
component: rotor_and_bearings
section: Compressor Operation & Thermal Limits
---
TypeB high-pressure compressors utilize tilting-pad hydrodynamic journal bearings and a balance drum.
Normal operating temperature is between 55°C and 75°C. Continuous operation above 85°C initiates babbitt metal softening and wipe risk.
Vibration baseline across X/Y radial probes should not exceed 0.8 mm/s in good health state.
Vibration levels reaching 1.5 mm/s require immediate operational de-rating and seal buffer gas pressure checks.

---
title: TypeC Industrial Helical Gearbox Manual
machine_type: TypeC
document_type: manual
component: gearbox_and_pinion
section: Gearbox Lubrication & Mesh Alignment
---
The TypeC helical speed reducer requires synthetic gear oil (ISO VG 220 or VG 320).
Nominal operating oil sump temperature is 60°C to 80°C. Peak casing temperature must not exceed 90°C.
Vibration signature in healthy helical reducers shows predominant gear mesh frequencies with overall vibration RMS under 1.1 mm/s.
Elevated axial vibration (Z-axis) exceeding 1.3 mm/s indicates thrust bearing degradation or improper gear tooth contact pattern.

title: TypeD Direct Drive Induction Motor Manual
machine_type: TypeD
document_type: manual
component: motor_windings_and_bearings
section: Electric Motor Bearing Specifications
---
TypeD three-phase squirrel-cage induction motors are fitted with deep-groove ball bearings on the non-drive end and cylindrical roller bearings on the drive end.
Maximum continuous operating temperature for Class F insulation is 95°C measured on the stator core.
Overall vibration velocity should remain below 1.0 mm/s. If radial vibration (X or Y axis) exceeds 1.4 mm/s, check for rotor dynamic unbalance, eccentric air gap, or broken rotor bars.

title: TypeE Reciprocating Utility Compressor Manual
machine_type: TypeE
document_type: manual
component: crosshead_and_crankshaft
section: Reciprocating Dynamics & Crankcase Baseline
---
TypeE units exhibit inherent reciprocating inertia pulses. Baseline vibration limits are higher than centrifugal machinery.
Acceptable healthy vibration is up to 1.8 mm/s RMS on the horizontal frame (X-axis).
If vibration magnitude exceeds 2.5 mm/s accompanied by sudden crankcase temperature rise above 85°C, stop the unit immediately to inspect crosshead pin clearances and connecting rod big-end bearings.
