title: Case Log 2024-TXM-014: Bearing Thermal Runaway on TypeA
machine_type: TypeA
machine_id: TXM-014
document_type: case_study
component: bearing
failure_type: bearing_wear
section: Case Summary TXM-014
---
Machine TXM-014 (TypeA pump) operated at high load (88%) for 180 continuous hours.
Observation: Vibration_x climbed from 0.45 mm/s to 1.35 mm/s over 36 hours while casing temperature escalated from 62°C to 84°C.
RUL prediction dropped below 8 hours with Health State transitioning from Warning to Critical.
Action Taken: Emergency planned shutdown executed under P1 Priority.
Root Cause: Bearing outer raceway showed advanced spalling and grease washout from a damaged labyrinth seal.
Resolution: Replaced drive-end bearing with SKF Explorer 6312-2Z and installed new fluoroelastomer seal. Vibration dropped to 0.32 mm/s post-restart.

---
title: Case Log 2024-TXM-029: Coupling Misalignment on TypeC Reducer
machine_type: TypeC
machine_id: TXM-029
document_type: case_study
component: shaft_coupling
failure_type: misalignment
section: Case Summary TXM-029
---
Machine TXM-029 experienced persistent Z-axis axial vibration reaching 1.48 mm/s, while radial X/Y vibration remained moderately stable at 0.9 mm/s.
Health state was flagged as Warning with moderate degradation slope.
Action Taken: Technicians scheduled alignment inspection during shift changeover (P3 Priority, within 48 hours).
Root Cause: Thermal expansion of driven compressor caused 0.18 mm vertical thermal growth, throwing coupling into angular misalignment.
Resolution: Pre-shimmed motor base with 0.15 mm cold offset. Operating vibration Z-axis normalized to 0.41 mm/s.
